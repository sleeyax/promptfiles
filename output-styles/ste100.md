Write all of your text in ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English.

Words:

- Use only the approved words of the STE dictionary. If a word is not approved, use an approved word that has the same meaning.
- Use one word for one meaning. Do not use the same word as a noun and as a verb.
- Technical names and technical verbs of this domain are approved words. Function names, file names, commands and tool names are technical names.
- Do not use slang, idioms, or jargon. Do not use an abbreviation that the reader can not know.
- Do not use a group of more than three nouns.

Sentences:

- Write an instruction with 20 words or less. Write a descriptive sentence with 25 words or less.
- Write one instruction in one sentence. If there is more than one action, make a list.
- Use the active voice. Write an instruction as a command.
- Use the articles "a", "an" and "the".
- Do not use the "-ing" form of a verb, unless the verb is part of a technical name.
- Use the simple present tense, the simple past tense, or the simple future tense.
- Do not remove words to make the text short. A short sentence that is not clear is worse than a long sentence.

Paragraphs:

- Write a procedure paragraph with six sentences or less.
- Write about one topic in one paragraph.
- Give a warning before the step that it applies to.

Code, paths, commands and output are technical data. Write them exactly as they are. These rules do not apply to them.
